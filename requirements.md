Based on the Jira tickets tracked in PAICE Studio (STUDIOPE), here are the detailed functional and technical requirements for Course Outline Generation. Overview & ScopeThe Course Outline Generation feature (https://pearson.atlassian.net/browse/STUDIOPE-6, https://pearson.atlassian.net/browse/STUDIOPE-93) converts approved Learning Goals into structured course containers (Modules, Lessons, Units) aligned with standards, assessment strategies, and instructional load constraints.1. Structural Hierarchy & RelationshipsHierarchy: Course Outline ➔ Unit ➔ Lesson ➔ Module ➔ Slate1:1 Learning Goal Mapping: Every generated Learning Goal (LG) correlates to exactly 1 Module.Slates: Modules are subdivided into Slates (single pages of content).Grouping: Modules are grouped into Lessons, which are subsequently grouped into Units.2. Learning Design Inputs (https://pearson.atlassian.net/browse/STUDIOPE-91)Before generating the outline, the system captures user preferences across several design dimensions:Unit-to-Unit Progression:Theme-Based: Groups lessons into units by theme or big ideas.Standards-Driven: Organizes units in the exact sequence standards appear in the framework.Chronological: Groups lessons and units historically/chronologically (e.g., History/Social Studies).Skills-Based: Progresses from foundational/simple skills to complex skills.In-Unit Learning Flow:Dynamic Classroom Instructional Model (DCIM): Follows the standard PVS sequence: Introduce ➔ Understand ➔ Apply ➔ Reflect ➔ Evaluate.Instructional Load Calculator:Inputs: Minutes per Lesson Day (e.g., 45 mins), Lessons scheduled per week (M–F selection), and Total Course Duration (in weeks).Outputs: Calculates Total Course Hours and Total Lesson Days.Constraint: Lessons must fit within the daily minute allowance and total calculated lesson days across semesters.Effort Configuration: Captures ratings (1–5) for Overall Effort, Grading Effort, and Teaching Effort.3. Assessment Design & Placement (https://pearson.atlassian.net/browse/STUDIOPE-92, https://pearson.atlassian.net/browse/STUDIOPE-8)Applies a default assessment strategy mapped directly to DCIM lesson types:Lesson / Container TypeAssociated Assessment TypeScoring & DeliveryUnderstand LessonQuick CheckAuto-scored, multiple choice (1 per Understand lesson)Apply LessonSample WorkTeacher-scored, dropbox submissionReview LessonUnit Online PracticeAuto-scored, multiple choiceTest LessonUnit TestAuto-scored & teacher-scored (1 per Unit)Semester ReviewSemester Online PracticeAuto-scored, multiple choiceSemester ExamSemester ExamAuto-scored (1 per Semester)PortfolioPortfolioHuman-scored dropbox assignment (2–4 per Semester)4. Editing, Regeneration, & ReorganizationRegeneration (https://pearson.atlassian.net/browse/STUDIOPE-94):Full Regeneration: Regenerates the complete course outline based on updated prompts or preferences.Unit/Lesson Level Regeneration: Regenerates specific units or lessons while maintaining existing Learning Goal alignments and accepts user-guided prompts.Undo Support: Allows reverting regeneration actions (https://pearson.atlassian.net/browse/STUDIOPE-300).Manual Editing & CRUD Operations (https://pearson.atlassian.net/browse/STUDIOPE-95):Direct title editing for Units, Lessons, and Modules.Add Unit (at Course level), Add Lesson (at Unit level), or Add Special Modules (Portfolio, etc.).Delete Units/Lessons (restricted if containers are non-empty until modules are moved).Auto-save & draft preservation so progress is retained if navigating away.Drag-and-Drop Reorganization (https://pearson.atlassian.net/browse/STUDIOPE-96, https://pearson.atlassian.net/browse/STUDIOPE-210):Drag handles to reorder Units at course level, move Lessons within/across Units, and move Modules within/across Lessons with drop target validation.5. Visualization & Export CapabilitiesCourse Outline View: Standard hierarchical tree view (Accordion) displaying Units, Lessons, Modules, and Slates.Course Graph View (https://pearson.atlassian.net/browse/STUDIOPE-172):Standards-driven accordion view mapping Standards (blue bubble) ➔ Anchor Standards ➔ Modules (green bubble with LG number, Unit #, and Lesson #) to ensure 100% standard coverage.Excel Simple Course Map Export (https://pearson.atlassian.net/browse/STUDIOPE-97):Exports outline to PVS Excel format with columns: Unit #, Unit Name, Lesson #, Lesson Name, LO Type, Cypress Module, Assessment, Learning Goal, Standard Code, and Standard.6. Content Reuse Across State Courses (https://pearson.atlassian.net/browse/STUDIOPE-568)Compares target state standards with existing PAICE course standards for overlap.Recommends Learning Goal reuse for overlapping standards; reused LGs and content are locked from editing, while unique standards generate new LGs and course outline sections.
